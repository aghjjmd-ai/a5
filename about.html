About Tickers

Watches, without the jargon.

Tickers started as a notebook of answers to friends’ questions: will this fit me, why is it gaining time, can I swim in it? We turned those answers into a site.

We cover three things in depth: fit, accuracy and everyday wear. Our tools turn specifications into plain answers, and our guides explain the reasoning behind them.

Independent

We do not sell watches, take payment for coverage or use affiliate links.

Checked

Figures come from published specifications and standards, and each guide is reviewed by a second editor.

Practical

Every guide ends with something you can measure or try yourself.
